Privacy Policy

How Forma handles your number, your quote, and your job.

Forma connects homeowners who've already been quoted for home-service work with nearby providers willing to beat the price — over SMS and MMS, start to finish. This page covers what we collect and how messages move.

Effective October 10, 2026 Applies to Homeowners & service providers texting Forma Contact amang2@andrew.cmu.edu
In short

We collect your phone number and the message content you send us (what's broken, the address, a photo, a callback number) to match jobs with providers and process payment. We share only what each side needs to do business — your street address, for instance, goes to the winning provider and no one else. We don't sell your information.

1.1Who this covers

This policy applies to anyone who exchanges text messages with Forma's number: homeowners shopping a quote, and the local service providers on our roster who bid on that work. "Forma," "we," and "us" refer to the operators of this service.

1.2What we collect

  • Your phone number — from the SMS/MMS network, the moment you text us.
  • What you tell us — the work you were quoted, the price, the company that quoted you, your address, a callback number, and a photo of the problem if you send one.
  • Provider bids — the price a provider offers to beat a quote, sent back over SMS.
  • Payment details — handled directly by Stripe, our payment processor. We never see or store your card number.

We don't collect anything beyond what a normal text conversation requires — no location tracking, no device fingerprinting, no browsing data, because there's no app or website account involved.

1.3How we use it

  • To match your job with nearby providers and collect competing prices.
  • To release your address to the provider you choose — and only that provider, and only once you've chosen.
  • To generate and send a payment link once work is marked complete.
  • To reply to you conversationally — a language model reads your messages to figure out what's missing and what to ask next.

1.4Who we share it with

Running a two-sided marketplace over text means a few specialized services see parts of a conversation in order to make it work. Here's exactly who, and why:

Twilio
Delivers and receives every SMS/MMS message — they see the message content and phone numbers involved, same as any carrier or messaging provider.
Anthropic
Processes message text (and photos, when you send one) to understand the job and draft a reply. Not used to train their models on your data.
Stripe
Handles payment and payout once a job is booked — they receive the price and the parties' contact details needed to move the money.
The other party
A provider you book receives your address, phone number, and job details. Providers never see each other's bids or identities.

We don't sell information to anyone, and we don't share it for advertising.

1.5SMS/MMS program details

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies with how active your job is — typically a handful of messages from first contact to booking. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for support. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

1.6How long we keep it

We keep job records for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet payment and tax obligations. Photos and conversation history are tied to the specific job they relate to.

1.7Your choices

Reply STOP to a Forma message at any time to opt out of future texts. To request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it, email us — see Contact below.

1.8Children's privacy

Forma is a service for arranging home-repair work and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

1.9Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information in a conversation, but no method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

1.10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a material way, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Forma after a change means you accept the update.
