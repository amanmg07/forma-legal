The agreement between Forma, homeowners, and providers.

Forma introduces homeowners and service providers and handles the back-and-forth over text. Here's what each side agrees to.

Effective October 10, 2026 Applies to Homeowners & service providers texting Forma Contact amang2@andrew.cmu.edu
In short

Forma introduces homeowners and service providers and handles the back-and-forth over text — we're not the ones doing the plumbing. We take a commission when a job books through us. Providers are independent businesses, not Forma employees, and homeowners are responsible for confirming a provider's licensing before work begins.

2.1Acceptance

By texting Forma's number, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, simply don't text us — or reply STOP at any time to stop.

2.2What Forma does

Forma is a marketplace, not a plumbing company. We take an existing quote, ask nearby providers whether they can beat it, present you the results, and — if you book — pass your details to the winning provider and handle payment on their behalf, keeping a commission. The actual work is performed entirely by the provider you choose.

2.3Eligibility

  • You must be at least 18 and able to enter a binding agreement.
  • You need a valid U.S. mobile number capable of receiving SMS/MMS.
  • Providers represent that they are appropriately licensed and insured for the work they bid on, where their trade requires it. Forma does not independently verify provider licensing, and homeowners are responsible for confirming this themselves before work begins.

2.4For homeowners

  • The quote details you provide (scope of work, price, company) should be accurate — providers are bidding against real numbers.
  • Forma doesn't guarantee a better price will be found. If nobody beats your quote, we'll tell you.
  • Once you book a provider, the agreement to perform the work is between you and that provider. Forma facilitates the introduction and payment; we aren't a party to the work itself.

2.5For providers

  • Bids should be genuine — a price you're actually prepared to honor if chosen.
  • You're an independent business, not an employee, contractor, or agent of Forma.
  • Forma's commission is deducted from the agreed price automatically when payment is collected, as communicated to you when a job books.

2.6How providers join — and consent to SMS

Providers aren't self-service signups. Each one is recruited directly by Forma, by phone or in person, before being added to the active roster. The same conversation is where they agree to receive job-lead texts:

"Hi, this is [Forma team member] with Forma — we're a service that sends local plumbing and home-service providers text message leads for jobs homeowners want shopped for a better price. We'd like to send job-lead texts to this number when relevant work comes up nearby. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to cancel at any time. Do you agree to receive these texts?"

Only providers who agree are added to the roster. Message and data rates may apply; message frequency depends on how many relevant jobs come up.

2.7Payment

Payment is processed through Stripe. Once work is marked complete, the homeowner receives a payment link for the agreed price; the provider's share and Forma's commission are settled automatically. If something's wrong with the work, reply ISSUE instead of paying, and we'll hold the payment while it's sorted out.

2.8No guarantee of work quality

Forma facilitates an introduction and a payment, nothing more. We don't inspect, supervise, or guarantee the quality of any work performed. Any dispute about the work itself is between the homeowner and the provider.

2.9Limitation of liability

Forma is provided "as is." To the extent allowed by law, Forma isn't liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from use of the service, including damages related to work performed by a provider found through Forma.

2.10Ending the relationship

Either side can stop at any time — reply STOP, or simply stop responding. We may also remove a provider from our roster or decline to serve a homeowner at our discretion, including for abusive or fraudulent behavior.

2.11Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as Forma grows. Material changes will be reflected in the date at the top of this page.
